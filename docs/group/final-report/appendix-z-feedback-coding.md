### Z.2 The categories, fixed before coding

| Code | Category | A unit goes here when it is about… | Where the category comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Attempt first | the student writing their own answer before help, or what that did for them | DG1 |
| B | Help when stuck | hints, questions, scaffolding, the Show answer path, whether the help moved them on | DG2 |
| C | Grounding in the material | what the help draws on, and seeing that material in the app | DG3 |
| D | Amount to read | the length of replies, too much on screen at once | DG4 |
| E | Own answer and credit | what counts as correct, and what progress is given for | DG5 |
| F | Trust in the verdict | what a verdict rests on, whether it is right, seeing the answer key | The need for feedback that the student can check (Section 3.2, finding 3) |
| G | The two agents | who Ellie and Max are, why there are two, labels that name them | The teaching team's feedback on complexity |
| I | Cost in time | the time or effort that the approach takes | The assistance dilemma (Koedinger & Aleven, 2007) |
| H | Interface mechanics | how the screen behaves, apart from any design goal | Remainder |
| J | Beyond scope | a feature outside the tutorial workspace | Remainder |

Direction: **+** supports the design, **−** names a problem, **R** asks for a change.
